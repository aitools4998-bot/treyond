Refund & Returns

Last updated: May 2025

1. Return Window

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unused, unwashed, and in original packaging with all tags intact.

2. Non-Returnable Items

Customized or stitched uniforms, altered garments, and items purchased during sale are not eligible for return.

3. How to Initiate a Return

Contact us via WhatsApp at +91 95121 02102 or email at support@treyondworld.com with your order ID and reason for return. We will arrange a pickup.

4. Refund Process

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, your refund will be processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

5. Exchange Policy

We offer free size exchanges subject to availability. Contact us within 7 days of delivery to request an exchange.

6. Damaged or Wrong Items

If you received a damaged or incorrect item, please contact us within 48 hours with photos. We will replace it at no extra cost.
