Shipping Policy

Last updated: May 2025

1. Free Shipping

We offer free shipping on all orders above ₹2,999 across India. Orders below ₹2,999 are charged a flat shipping fee of ₹99.

2. Delivery Time

Standardery takes 5-7 business days. Express delivery (2-3 business days) is available at checkout for select pincodes.

3. Order Processing

Orders are processed within 1-2 business days. You will receive a tracking link via SMS and email once your order is shipped.

4. Pan India Delivery

We deliver to all states and union territories across India. Remote areas may take 2-3 additional days.

5. Bulk Orders

For bulk uniform orders (10+ pieces), please contact us on WhatsApp at +91 95121 02102 for special pricing and delivery arrangements.

6. Tracking Your Order

Once shipped, you can track your order using the tracking link sent to your registered mobile number or email.
